Frequently Asked Questions

How long does water damage restoration take in Jacksonville?

The duration of water damage restoration can vary based on the extent of the damage. Typically, a thorough restoration can take anywhere from a few days to a week. Our team assesses each situation to provide a clear timeline.

What to do after water damage in Jacksonville?

If you experience water damage, it's crucial to turn off the water source, remove any valuables, and contact a professional service immediately. Quick action can prevent further damage and mold growth.

How can I prevent water damage in my Jacksonville home?

Regular maintenance, such as checking for leaks, cleaning gutters, and ensuring proper drainage, can help prevent water damage. Installing sump pumps and using mold-resistant materials can also be beneficial.

What are the signs of water damage in my home?

Signs of water damage include discoloration on walls or ceilings, musty odors, peeling paint, and visible mold growth. If you notice any of these signs, it's essential to address them promptly.

What is the difference between water mitigation and restoration?

Mitigation stops further damage (extraction, drying, demolition of ruined materials). Restoration rebuilds what was removed (drywall, flooring, paint). We handle both phases so you deal with one team. Call (904) 663-0696 to start.

How long does full water damage restoration take?

Mitigation typically takes 3 to 7 days. Full restoration including reconstruction takes 2 to 6 weeks depending on damage extent. We provide a timeline after the initial assessment.

Should I file an insurance claim for water damage?

For sudden events like burst pipes or storm damage, usually yes. Compare the repair cost against your deductible first. We document everything to support your claim and work with adjusters regularly.
