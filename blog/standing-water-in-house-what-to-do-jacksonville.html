Standing Water in Your House Right Now? Do This First

Call (904) 663-0696 — 24/7 Response

Water is standing in your house. Do these things in this order — safety, source, photos, extraction — before you worry about anything else.

Standing water is an emergency, not a cleanup project. Every hour it sits, it soaks deeper into floors, walls, and subfloor, and Jacksonville humidity keeps it wet long after the source stops.

1. Safety First

If water is near outlets, appliances, or your breaker panel, do not wade in — turn off power to the area if you can do so safely from a dry spot, and stay out of electrified water. If sewage is involved, treat it as contaminated: keep children and pets away and do not mop it around.

2. Stop the Source, Then Photograph

Turn off the main water supply if the source is plumbing. If it is storm water, sandbag or divert what you can safely. Then photograph everything — water levels, damaged rooms, the source if visible — before anything is moved. Photos are your claim evidence.

3. Get Extraction Today

Call for emergency extraction the same day. Mopping handles surface water; extraction and drying handle what soaked in. Waiting until tomorrow in Jacksonville humidity is how a one-room flood becomes a whole-house mold job.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I wait for the water to recede on its own?

No. Standing water soaks deeper every hour. Get extraction today.

Is it safe to walk through standing water?

Not if it may be electrified or contaminated (sewage). When in doubt, stay out and call professionals.

What should I photograph?

Water levels, every damaged room, the source, and damaged belongings — before cleanup moves anything.
